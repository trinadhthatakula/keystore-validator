Based on SSLMate go-pkcs12 v0.7.1 (BSD license). The local fork adds certificate-only inspection, preserves alias and key-ID metadata without decrypting shrouded key bags, and verifies the store MAC before returning certificates. It applies limits to untrusted input, KDF cost, salts, ASN.1 containers and CBC padding, rejects missing MACs and ambiguous associations, supports UTF-16 password and alias decoding, and retries OpenJDK's legacy seven-bit PBE encoding after a verified MAC if normal legacy decryption fails. PBKDF2 uses the Go 1.24 standard library. Encrypted SafeContents must be decrypted to reach certificate bags; individual shrouded private keys are not decrypted.
